# taxonomy_core.txt — the animate ontology backbone.
#
# The per-subject corpora connect animals to "living thing" (animal isa living thing),
# but the HUMAN branch was never linked: person / child / children had no path to
# "living thing". That gap made selectional-constraint learning collapse — a verb seen
# with both people and animals found no shared animate supertype, so "eat" over-fit to
# "person" and rejected "monkey". These edges close the gap: humans and animals now share
# "living thing", giving verb learning a real animate category to generalise to.
#
# These are genuine ISA facts (a person IS a living thing), not a hack — they belong in
# the knowledge base. Kept in one file so the backbone is auditable in one place.

ISA: person | living being
ISA: person | living thing
ISA: child | person
ISA: children | person
ISA: people | person
ISA: animals | animal
ISA: living beings | living thing
ISA: animal | living being
ISA: plant | living being
